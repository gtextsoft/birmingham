The Invitation

This is not a conference. There are no crowded exhibition halls and no rehearsed sales pitches — only one room, and the right leaders in it.

The Birmingham Meet & Greet exists for the conversations that never happen over email and cannot be faked at scale: strategic introductions, frank counsel on cross-border capital, and partnerships that quietly shift the trajectory of a company.

Why This Room

Engineered for high-leverage connection

Every element of the morning is chosen to make introductions meaningful, productive, and lasting.

A Vetted, Balanced Room

02 / Candour

Off-the-Record by Design

Conducted under Chatham House principles. Unvarnished dialogue on capital deployment, scaling across borders, and operating in turbulent macroeconomic climates.

Your Host

Founder · SACI Holdings · Stephen Akintayo Foundation

Dr. Stephen Akintayo is a Forbes Best of Africa honouree, serial investor, and mentor to a rising generation of founders scaling across Africa, Europe, and the United Kingdom. Through SACI Holdings and the Stephen Akintayo Foundation, he brings together institutional capital, pioneering ventures, and high-trust leadership.

In Birmingham, he convenes this circle in person — a closed salon designed to put you in the room with decision-makers who can genuinely change the scale of what you are building.
